# podcrates v0.51.0

The fifty-first release of podcrates. It carries part 1 of `pod-egress`
(step 2 of the move of podssh, PodUtils/podssh#248), the fixes of
PodUtils/podnet#572, PodUtils/podvm#380 and PodUtils/podssh#253, and the
asks of PodUtils/podvm#389, #390 and #393.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-egress` | New. Part 1 of the roads out of podssh-ws at podssh `2ffb7cd7`: via (HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2 proxies), the forwarders, Wisp, wstunnel, meek_lite, WebTunnel, the Access client of Cloudflare; each road behind its own feature (T-035, partial; calls C616 to C620) | podssh |
| `pod-link` | Fix: a closed half of a split session gives `Lost` and not "the carrier of the link ended" (T-639, PodUtils/podnet#572) | podnet |
| `pod-guest` | Fix: the agent of a Windows guest tries its shutdown again and waits when `ping` fails; a run that times out keeps its evidence (`Settings::evidence`, the module `evidence`) (T-640, PodUtils/podvm#380) | podvm |
| `pod-quic` | Fix: the socket of an endpoint (`on_socket`, `over`) ends after 256 failed reads in a row (`bounded::Bounded`, `FAILED_READS_MOST`), so a way that fails each read no longer spins a core and the drop of the endpoint returns (T-643, PodUtils/podssh#253) | podssh |
| `pod-hostprobe` | New module `cgroup`: the leg of a delegated cgroup2 subtree, with no root (`probe`, `assess`, `Delegation`, `Reason`) (T-648, PodUtils/podvm#390) | podvm |
| `pod-qga` | New call `Client::osinfo` (`guest-get-osinfo`, `wire::OsInfo`) (T-650, PodUtils/podvm#393) | podvm |
| `pod-services` | New service `pkgforge-qemu`: the static QEMU 9.2.0 of pkgforge's bincache at `api.ghcr.pkgforge.dev`, with podvm's pins and a watch of each file (T-647, PodUtils/podvm#389) | podvm |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.51.0`.

- podssh: apply `adoption/podssh/pod-egress.patch` at `2ffb7cd7` (its
  `.commit`) after the patches of `v0.50.0`; its `.run` names the checks.
  The modules `via/`, `forwarder/`, `wstunnel/`, `wisp/`, `meek/` and the
  files `meek.rs`, `webtunnel.rs`, `pt.rs`, `cloudflare.rs` of podssh-ws
  become glue over `pod-egress`; their tests, `forwarder-live.yml` and the
  plants t438, t439 and t441 leave podssh. Drop the ignore of
  `quic_socket_fails.rs` (T-643).
- podnet, podvm, podrunner: the bump of the tag.

## Breaking

None for an existing crate.

## Known defect

- `pod-guest`: the installer and `dos::run` do not yet have the fixes of
  T-640 (T-641, open).
- `pod-egress`: parts 2 and 3 (VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, EICE,
  IAP) are open (T-035, partial).
- `pod-netlab`: five value sets of sand-net have no source (T-586,
  partial).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
