# podcrates v0.67.0

The sixty-seventh release of podcrates. It carries two asks of podssh
(a renewal against the default relay, a route to a Unix socket), one of
podnet (the remote port of localhost.run), a fix of
the node of the reverse road for a local side that reads, a durable
replace of a file, the first parts of the findings of the dig study and of
the ingest study, and a live measurement of the default relay.

## Tests and checks

- `scripts/check-loopback.py`: no bind of a fixed loopback port in product code (T-831).
- `scripts/check-private-replace.py`: no rename of a file outside the helper of T-192.
- `scripts/check-results.py`: the `RESULTS.md` of each measured crate (`pod-link`, `pod-ws`; T-824).
- `pod-e2e`: a seal and an open each make one allocation for each record (`tests/allocations.rs`).
- `pod-link`: the checksum of each of the 65,536 words against a second form; a test that fails and names each carrier whose feature is off.
- `pod-relay`: `tests/routes_unix.rs`, a burst to a local side that reads, and the live measurement of the default relay (`live.yml`, `relay: live_measure`; `docs/research/relay-rates.md`).
- New plants: `proxy-family-no-hold`, `relay-unix-route-as-tcp`, `acme-replace-by-rename`, `identity-rotate-twice`, `relay-stall-at-first-mib`; `acme-store-not-private` and `core-store-shares-one-temp-name` planted again on the helper.

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.67.0`; `issue-sync.yml@v0.67.0` takes `podcrates_ref: v0.67.0`.

- podssh: a route `unix-connect:PATH` can be `Route::unix(name, PATH)` handed to `Routes::open_with`; the opener of its own (`node_unix_open.rs`) can go. Its `--caller-line` writes its line on the stream that `open_with` gives. A renewal against the default relay gives no renewal, not an error.
- podnet: move the copies of the replace of a private file onto `pod_core::files::replace_private` (six in podnet, with PodUtils/podnet#431 and PodUtils/podnet#433); a plain node of the reverse road keeps a session whose local side reads slower than a burst.
- podssh: move its two copies of the replace and the `sync_dir` of its spent-grant record onto the helper.
- Each: the plan by family of `pod-proxy` and the rotation of `pod-identity` wait for a measurement and a consumer; they change nothing until a caller sets them.

## Breaking

- None. `pod_proxy::Settings` has a new public field (`families`); a consumer that builds it with `Settings::new` takes it with no change.

## Known defect

- `pod-session`: the cause of the stalled resume of PodUtils/podssh#283 is open (T-767, partial); a spare waits 20 s when a full link holds the writer (T-768, open).
- `pod-enter`: the ids of the plan do nothing for a caller that is root of the initial namespace (T-791, open, when a consumer asks).
- `pod-devtunnel`: no device-code flow and no token store yet (T-378, partial).
- The nightly: armv7 (T-789), riscv64 (T-766).
- `pod-iroh`: the relay dial takes a proxy URL, not the caller's dialer (T-809).
- `pod-link`: the tcp carrier allocates about 3 times for each `DATA` frame, in its TLS (T-798).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).
- The seeds of the pool of the default relay make no pairs of the reverse road; the live measurement measures the default host alone (T-812, partial).
