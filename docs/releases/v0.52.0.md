# podcrates v0.52.0

The fifty-second release of podcrates. It carries parts 2 and 3 of
`pod-egress` (step 2 of the move of podssh, PodUtils/podssh#248) and the
asks of PodUtils/podvm#395, #403, #406 and #407.

## Crates

| Crate | Change | Consumers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pod-egress` | Part 2: VLESS (a WebSocket and XHTTP), VMess's AEAD form, Trojan and Shadowsocks (the AEAD form and SIP022), each behind its feature and each a `pod_core::Dial` with a typed failure (T-035; calls C621 to C625); `vmess-interop.yml`. Part 3: EICE (AWS's EC2 Instance Connect Endpoint, SigV4) and IAP (Google Cloud's IAP for TCP; its carrier says its steps as events of the caller's `Note`), each behind its feature (T-035; calls C631 to C634) | podssh |

## How a consumer takes it

Each podcrates dependency names the tag `v0.52.0`.

- podssh: after `adoption/podssh/pod-egress.patch` (part 1, `v0.51.0`),
  apply `adoption/podssh/pod-egress-part2.patch` and
  `adoption/podssh/pod-egress-part3.patch` at `2ffb7cd7` (each `.needs`
  names part 1; each `.run` names the checks). `vless/`, `vmess/`,
  `trojan/`, `shadowsocks/`, `eice/` and `iap/` of podssh-ws become glue;
  their tests, the plant t440 and `vmess-interop.yml` leave podssh.
- podvm: the bump of the tag; `Plan { .., hostname: None }` where it
  builds a plan by hand.
- podnet, podrunner: the bump of the tag.

## Breaking

- `pod_enter::Plan` has the new field `hostname`: a plan built with a
  struct literal names it.
- The report of `pod-hostprobe` has a new census row; its schema
  (`schemas/hostprobe-report.v1.schema.json`) names it.

## Known defect

- `pod-egress`: a failed TLS handshake or upgrade on VLESS, VMess and
  Trojan is `WebSocket`/`Unavailable` (T-652, open).
- `pod-netlab`: five value sets of sand-net have no source (T-586,
  partial).
- `pod-gsr` on Windows ARM64: `STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION` (T-131, open).

## Publish

`publish.yml` runs on the tag: the dry run of `cargo publish --workspace`.
The switch `crates-io` of `Cargo.toml` is false (the owner, 2026-11-09).
